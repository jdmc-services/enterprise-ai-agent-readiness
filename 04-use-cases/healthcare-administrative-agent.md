# Use Case: Healthcare Administrative Agent

## Scope

This pattern supports nonclinical administrative workflows.

## Appropriate Examples

- Policy and procedure navigation
- Administrative intake
- Staff knowledge support
- Appointment preparation
- Service-request routing
- Operational communications
- Workforce support

## Excluded Examples

- Diagnosis
- Treatment recommendations
- Autonomous clinical decision-making
- Medication instructions
- Emergency triage
- Unsanctioned access to protected health information

## Governance

- Business Associate Agreement where applicable
- Minimum necessary access
- Human escalation
- Audit logging
- Privacy review
- Clinical boundary review
