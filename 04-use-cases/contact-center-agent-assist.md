# Use Case: Contact Center Agent Assist

## Business Problem

Agents may rely on fragmented knowledge, experience long search times, and perform excessive after-call documentation.

## Proposed Capability

- Real-time knowledge retrieval
- Interaction summarization
- Suggested responses
- Next-best action recommendations
- Quality and coaching insights
- Automated draft notes
- Human handoff

## Architecture Requirements

- Preserve existing telephony and routing
- Support resilient fallback
- Avoid emergency-call automation
- Maintain recording and consent controls
- Integrate with approved knowledge sources
- Log all recommendations and actions

## Metrics

- Average handle time
- Transfer rate
- First-contact resolution
- Hold time
- Abandonment
- After-call work
- Quality score
- Training time
