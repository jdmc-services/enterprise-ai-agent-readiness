# Workforce Enablement Plan

## Audience Tracks

### Executives

- AI value and risk
- Governance
- Investment decisions
- Accountability
- Metrics

### Managers

- Workflow redesign
- Human oversight
- Change management
- Performance measures
- Escalation

### Technical Teams

- Architecture
- Identity
- Integration
- Security
- Monitoring
- Incident response

### End Users

- Appropriate use
- Verification
- Data handling
- Escalation
- Feedback

## 30-Day Enablement Sequence

- Week 1: AI fundamentals and use-case selection
- Week 2: Security, governance, and data handling
- Week 3: Role-based workflow practice
- Week 4: Pilot measurement and improvement
