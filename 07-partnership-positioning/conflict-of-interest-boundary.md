# Conflict-of-Interest Boundary

Any discussion involving a current employer must remain separate from independent consulting, education, nonprofit, or commercial activity.

## Principles

- No compensation for influencing an employer procurement decision
- No promise of executive access
- No disclosure of confidential operational information
- No use of employer resources for outside business
- Formal procurement and review must be followed
- Independent agreements should expressly exclude the employer
- Opportunity attribution must not create an ethical conflict
- Employer name and logo may not be used without authorization
