# Independent Enterprise AI Partnership Meeting Brief

## Purpose

Use this brief to evaluate independent advisory, architecture, implementation, education, publishing, and strategic-partnership opportunities with enterprise AI providers and professional-services organizations.

The objective is to identify a formal opportunity based on expertise, public intellectual property, and independently deliverable value—not access to a current employer.

---

## Positioning Statement

**James D. McClain, MBA connects enterprise AI strategy to the infrastructure, cybersecurity, governance, measurement, and workforce disciplines required to make transformation operationally credible.**

His value is the ability to translate between:

* Executive strategy
* Enterprise infrastructure
* Cloud architecture
* Cybersecurity and Zero Trust
* AI-agent governance
* Service management
* Contact-center operations
* Regulated-industry requirements
* Workforce adoption
* Servant leadership
* Measurable business outcomes

---

## Meeting Objectives

1. Understand what prompted the organization’s interest.
2. Determine which capabilities or public artifacts created the strongest alignment.
3. Identify opportunities that are independent of any current employer.
4. Clarify whether the potential role is advisory, technical, educational, commercial, or employment-based.
5. Define the decision-makers, commercial structure, deliverables, and success criteria.
6. Establish one ethical and independently executable next step.
7. Confirm that no opportunity depends on confidential information, employer influence, or unauthorized access.

---

## Professional Boundary Statement

Use this statement when employer access, internal information, procurement influence, or organizational introductions enter the conversation:

> My independent work must remain completely separate from my current employment. I cannot provide confidential information, internal metrics, private architecture, privileged introductions, procurement influence, or commitments on behalf of any employer. I am available to discuss independent advisory, architecture, education, publishing, and implementation opportunities based on my public frameworks and professional expertise.

## Questions to Ask

### Strategic Interest

1. What specifically about my experience or public portfolio led to this conversation?
2. Which business or technical problem do you believe I am best positioned to help solve?
3. Is your interest primarily strategic, technical, commercial, educational, or talent-related?
4. Which industries, customer segments, or operating environments are priorities?
5. What capability is currently missing from your team or partner ecosystem?

### Engagement Model

6. Do you see the strongest fit as an adviser, architect, consultant, implementation partner, educator, author, speaker, channel partner, or employee?
7. Would the engagement be with me individually, through an independent business entity, or through another formal structure?
8. What deliverables would define the engagement?
9. Who owns the budget and final decision?
10. What compensation structure is normally used for this type of work?

### Scope and Outcomes

11. What outcome should be visible within the first 30 to 60 days?
12. Which baseline metrics would be available?
13. What data, systems, integrations, and stakeholders would be involved?
14. What security, privacy, legal, and compliance reviews are required?
15. How would success be measured?
16. What would cause the organization to pause or stop the work?

### Independence and Ethics

17. Can this opportunity proceed without involving or depending on my current employer?
18. Can all discovery information be authorized and appropriately sanitized?
19. Are there any exclusivity, conflict-of-interest, intellectual-property, or noncompete requirements?
20. Who will confirm the ethics and contractual boundaries before work begins?

## Recommended 30-to-60-Day First Engagement

A strong initial engagement should:

* Be independent of any current employer
* Solve one clearly defined problem
* Use authorized and sanitized information
* Produce concrete deliverables
* Establish measurable success criteria
* Include security and governance review
* Include a workforce-adoption component
* Clarify intellectual-property ownership
* Use a written statement of work
* Create a decision point for a larger engagement

Example:

> Conduct a vendor-neutral enterprise AI-agent readiness assessment for one administrative workflow, including use-case intake, risk classification, reference architecture, human-approval controls, baseline metrics, workforce requirements, and an executive pilot recommendation.

## Commercial Questions

Before agreeing to proceed, clarify:

* Contracting entity
* Scope and deliverables
* Timeline
* Sponsor and decision-maker
* Payment structure
* Expense policy
* Intellectual-property ownership
* Confidentiality requirements
* Public-attribution rights
* Use of name, biography, image, or logo
* Exclusivity
* Conflict-of-interest review
* Data-access requirements
* Security requirements
* Termination rights
* Liability and insurance requirements

Professional legal and tax advice should be obtained before executing a material agreement.

---

## Red Flags

Pause the conversation if the opportunity depends on:

* Access to a current employer
* Influence over procurement
* Confidential information
* Internal metrics or architecture
* Unapproved introductions
* Employer systems, credentials, or resources
* Informal payment
* Unclear intellectual-property ownership
* Uncompensated production work disguised as discovery
* Broad exclusivity
* Undefined deliverables
* Pressure to bypass ethics, legal, procurement, privacy, or security review
* Claims that cannot be supported by public or authorized evidence

---

## Closing Questions

End the meeting by asking:

1. What do you believe is the strongest independent opportunity?
2. Who else must participate in the decision?
3. What specific deliverable should come first?
4. What commercial structure is appropriate?
5. What ethical, legal, security, or conflict review is required?
6. What is the next action, who owns it, and when will it occur?

## Decision

After the meeting, select one:

* **Proceed** — clear independent value, sponsor, structure, and next step
* **Clarify** — potential value exists, but scope or commercial terms remain incomplete
* **Refer** — the opportunity belongs with another provider or specialist
* **Decline** — the opportunity depends on inappropriate access, influence, or information
