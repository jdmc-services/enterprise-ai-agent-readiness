# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: professional, direct, and respectful.

- Critique content, not people. Strong disagreement about controls, architecture, or method is welcome — that is how frameworks improve.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- No disclosure of any organization's confidential information in issues, PRs, or discussions — including your own employer's.
- Assume good faith; write as if the person you're answering will be your colleague next year.

Violations may result in moderated or removed content and restricted participation. Concerns may be raised privately with the maintainer through the repository profile.
