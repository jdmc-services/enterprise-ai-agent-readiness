# Engagement Ethics and Independence

Trust is the product. These commitments govern every advisory engagement and are shared with clients at the outset.

## Independence

- **Vendor neutrality is structural.** No compensation, commission, or consideration from any platform vendor whose selection an engagement could influence. Where a vendor relationship exists on unrelated work, it is disclosed in writing before the engagement begins.
- **Recommendations trace to evidence.** Every platform-relevant finding maps to the framework's assessment criteria — reproducible by the client from their own data.

## Employer Separation

For any consultant who also holds an employed role, the boundary is absolute:

- No compensation of any kind for influencing an employer's procurement decision
- No promise, sale, or implication of access to an employer's executives, data, or decisions
- No disclosure of any employer's confidential information — operational, architectural, or commercial
- No use of employer time, systems, or resources for independent work
- Independent agreements expressly exclude the employer from scope
- An employer's name, logo, and affiliation are never used in independent marketing without written authorization
- Where an independent opportunity originates near an employer relationship, the formal procurement process governs — fully, without shortcuts, with the consultant recused from the employer's side of any related decision

## Client Protections

- Client confidential information is never reused across clients — frameworks travel, facts do not
- All examples in public materials are synthetic; no client is identifiable without written consent
- Conflicts discovered mid-engagement are disclosed within one business day, with a proposed resolution or withdrawal

These are not aspirations; they are engagement terms.
