# Advisory Service Offerings

Structured, fixed-scope engagements that apply this framework. Each produces named deliverables and a decision — not a slide deck that defers one.

## 1. Enterprise AI Readiness Assessment

**For:** organizations with AI interest, vendor pressure, and no governed path from one to the other.
**Duration:** 3–4 weeks.

Deliverables: use-case inventory with intake scoring · risk classification per candidate ([method](../03-security-governance/ai-risk-assessment.md)) · current-state architecture and integration review · data and knowledge-source inventory · governance gap analysis with prioritized roadmap · recommended first pilot with pre-committed success criteria · conservative ROI model.

**Exit decision enabled:** which use case, on what controls, measured how.

## 2. AI-Enabled Help Desk Accelerator

**For:** service-desk operations evaluating agent assist without disrupting the ITSM platform.
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks through pilot design.

Deliverables: 30-day baseline instrumentation · knowledge-readiness audit with gap map · ticket workflow and routing analysis · agent-assist design per the [reference architecture](../02-architecture/reference-architecture.md) · approval matrix and Zero-Trust control configuration · pilot plan with scorecard.

## 3. Contact Center AI Readiness Assessment

**For:** contact center leaders weighing agent assist against resilience, recording/consent, and quality obligations.
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks.

Deliverables: current-state voice/digital architecture review · agent-assist opportunity map with risk tiers · resilience and fallback design review · recording, consent, and privacy control verification · business case with measured-baseline ROI model · pilot design.

## 4. AI Governance and Agent Security Workshop

**For:** security and risk leaders extending Zero-Trust to nonhuman identities and agent tool use.
**Format:** 2-day intensive plus written deliverables.

Deliverables: AI system and nonhuman-identity inventory · agent threat model for the environment · Zero-Trust control mapping with gap findings · human approval matrix drafted for top use cases · AI incident-response playbook integrated with existing IR · tabletop exercise with findings memo · executive roadmap.

## 5. AI Workforce Academy

**For:** organizations that understand adoption fails at the desk.
**Format:** role-based curriculum, delivered or train-the-trainer.

Tracks: executive AI literacy and governance · frontline agent-assist proficiency with review discipline · supervisor coaching with assist signals · administrator and security operations · capstone tied to the organization's live pilot.

---

Every engagement is vendor-neutral: platform selection is an output of the assessment, never an assumption behind it.
