# Advisory Approach

## Operating Beliefs

**Adoption is an operations problem wearing a technology costume.** The binding constraints in enterprise AI are governance, workforce readiness, measurement discipline, and integration reality — not model capability. Advisory work that starts with the platform has started in the wrong place.

**Regulated environments are an advantage, not an obstacle.** Organizations forced to answer risk, privacy, and audit questions early build adoption that lasts. The framework treats compliance pressure as free rigor.

**The practitioner lens matters.** This framework reflects experience running mission-critical infrastructure, service desks, contact centers, and security programs — environments where "the AI was wrong" is not an acceptable incident summary. Recommendations are written to survive contact with a live operation.

## How Engagements Run

1. **Problems before platforms.** Intake and value hypotheses precede any vendor conversation.
2. **Evidence before enthusiasm.** Baselines are measured, criteria pre-committed, results scored against them — including the stop outcome.
3. **Controls before capability.** No agent reaches users before its risk tier, approval matrix, and incident path exist.
4. **People before scale.** Training and adoption work are scheduled deliverables, not change-management garnish.

## What Clients Can Expect

- Written deliverables that stand alone — usable after the engagement ends, by people who weren't in the room
- Direct findings, including unwelcome ones; a stopped bad pilot is a delivered outcome
- Fixed scopes with named exit decisions
- Full independence from platform vendors — compensation never depends on which product is selected
