## Decision Thresholds

- 8–12: Low risk
- 13–20: Moderate risk
- 21–30: High risk
- Above 30: Executive and risk committee review

## Mandatory Questions

- Can the agent expose restricted information?
- Can the agent make or trigger a consequential decision?
- Can an incorrect action be reversed?
- Is a human available to intervene?
- Is every action attributable and auditable?
